Wulf Quest for MSX2

A flip-screen jungle adventure: Ranger Vale alone in a 256-room jungle, looking for the four pieces of a broken amulet, with the Wulf on his trail.

A work in progress, built from the latest code: the whole game is in, and its tuning for real machines is still going on (the busiest rooms can run a little slow).

Play it in your browser

With WebMSX, an MSX emulator that runs in the browser. Pick a machine; the ROM loads straight from this site.

European MSX2 (50 Hz) Japanese MSX2 (60 Hz)

The game runs at the same speed on both. If WebMSX does not start it, open WebMSX, drop the downloaded ROM on its screen and choose the ASCII16 format.

Download

wulfquest.rom

A 512 KB ASCII16 MegaROM. It needs an MSX2, an MSX2+ or a turbo R (on an MSX1 it says so). Tagged versions, when there are some, are on the releases page.

Play it on your computer

openMSX

openMSX comes with C-BIOS, a free MSX BIOS, so it plays without any system ROM of your own:

openmsx -machine C-BIOS_MSX2_EU -carta wulfquest.rom -romtype ASCII16

Use C-BIOS_MSX2_JP for a 60 Hz machine, or any real MSX2 openMSX knows, if you have its system ROMs.

Other emulators

blueMSX, fMSX and the rest: load the ROM as a cartridge on an MSX2 machine and, if asked, choose the ASCII16 mapper.

A real MSX2

Write the ROM to a flash cartridge that can be an ASCII16 MegaROM of 512 KB. Saving needs a disk drive (see below).

Controls

Walkcursor keys (or Q A O P, in the options), or a joystick
Swing the sabreSpace or fire
MapM or the second button
PauseSTOP or F1
Back a stepESC

Saves

Three slots, saved every minute and from the pause menu, on a disk in the machine's drive; the last game is offered first on the title. Without a disk drive the game plays the same, without saves. In a browser emulator a save lasts only as long as its disk does.

About

This is the MSX2 version of Wulf Quest, played the same way, tick for tick: SCREEN 5, layered hardware sprites with a colour a line, the PSG for sound and music. Its source, and how to build it, is on GitHub.
